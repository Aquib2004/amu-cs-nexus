# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming community, we as contributors and maintainers of AMUCS Nexus pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity or background.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Showing empathy toward other community members when discussing mistakes.
- Focusing on technical substance over personal dispute.
- Helping others learn and succeed.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or other conduct that is unlawful or violates the rights of others.
- Trolling, insulting, or making ad hominem attacks.
- Publishing others private information without consent.
- Intentional disruption or deliberate introduction of defects.

## Our Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of behavior and for handling reports in a timely manner.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all spaces managed by the AMUCS Nexus project, including but not limited to code repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, and community discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and take appropriate action consistent with this code of conduct.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant (version 2.1), a widely used community standard.
